# Open the Hasad business pilot

The project files are complete for installation. Live availability depends on your domain, hosting database, SMS credentials and receiving-account details. No deployment or bank transaction has been made on your behalf.

## Configure the real business

1. Set the domain, HTTPS and a unique private configuration.
2. Create the super admin and named admin/support accounts.
3. Enter the actual operator name, address and support number; review the service/privacy text for the actual arrangements.
4. Set an actual hub address. The initial Sharkia hub is a proposed pilot, not an established physical facility.
5. Set Hasad's receiving InstaPay address/account or wallet. Customer collection and outgoing settlements remain through Hasad.
6. Validate proposed transport rates and the pickup threshold with the logistics team. Service fees start at zero.
7. Activate and test the production SMS service; keep provider usage/fraud controls appropriate for the pilot.

## Perform one real acceptance cycle

Use phone numbers and materials you control, and a small agreed transaction amount.

1. Submit a farmer request in Arabic without creating an account. Repeat with English to confirm language switching and form layout on your actual phones.
2. Sign in by SMS using the farmer's number and verify that the request appears.
3. Schedule collection or drop-off through Hasad. Inspect actual weight, record the internal report, confirm the seller authorization and receive a consignment batch.
4. Publish the batch from Inventory. A factory must see material/grade/quantity/price and the Hasad hub, with no farmer identity, phone, address or internal report.
5. Sign in as a separate factory buyer. Check the quote with both shipping modes. Buyer-arranged transport must add zero Hasad shipping.
6. Reserve a small quantity. Confirm that available stock falls and the stored quote does not change if a super admin changes future fees.
7. Make the agreed transfer in the actual bank/wallet app. Submit the reference. The order must remain under review, rather than becoming paid from the receipt alone.
8. Check the actual receiving account and approve the matching transfer as an admin. Attempting to approve it twice must be rejected.
9. Record dispatch and confirmed delivery. Check physical and reserved stock, the printable statement and balanced accounting entries.
10. Perform an agreed verified seller settlement and record it. The farmer must see their own sale value and transfer, without the factory identity or destination.
11. Test a separate cancellation/refund case. Paid cancellation before dispatch must leave a refund balance until verified refund transfers total the owed amount.
12. Sign in as support. Direct attempts to access financial/settings/report actions must fail, in addition to their links being absent.

## Publish only genuine availability

The production schema includes no demonstration transactions or default administrator password. The separate visual preview is marked as training data. Never import a development/test database as live stock. A database marked as training is blocked from production activation.

Review every live listing's actual quantity, seller authorization, price, hub and quality status. Physical grades are not feed-safety certification. Coordinate specialized buyer requirements through Hasad.

## Day-to-day operating controls

- Review new intake and expired reservation tickets.
- Confirm incoming transfers from the actual account; use unique statement references.
- Approve delivery before recognizing seller/carrier balances.
- Record verified outgoing transfers and partial outstanding balances.
- Keep identities and direct contacts inside the staff workflow, including manually supplied customer documents or support responses.
- Review stock movements, bank/held-fund/payable balances and project costs.
- Back up the database and private receipts, and periodically prove a restore in a separate environment.
