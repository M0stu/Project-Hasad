# Hasad identity and assets

Hasad / حصاد: “Good grows again” / “خير الأرض، من جديد”.

Deep agricultural green `#164A35`, warm ivory `#FAF9F3`, harvest gold `#E8B856`, and muted botanical tones form the identity. The interface uses an original scalable seed/cycle SVG mark, a consistent line-icon system, generous customer layouts and denser staff tables. Arabic is the primary language.

- `htdocs/assets/logo.svg`: original code-native seed and circular-growth mark; scalable and editable.
- `htdocs/assets/harvest.webp`: original generated editorial hero image, optimized as a WebP project asset.
- `htdocs/views/story.php` plus CSS/JS: native vector/CSS 30-second illustration of farm collection, Hasad coordination and factory delivery, with pause, chapter selection and reduced-motion support.

The hero is illustrative brand imagery, not evidence of an operating hub or a particular farm. It was created with the built-in imagegen tool and copied into the project as an asset. The selected prompt was:

> Create a beautiful wide landscape, professional editorial photograph of a cultivated Egyptian Nile Delta field just after harvest. Foreground: several round and rectangular golden straw bales with realistic fibrous texture; cultivated verdant crop rows behind, distant palm trees and a modest agricultural collection shed with a small loaded flatbed truck far in the background. Rich deep agrarian greens, warm natural gold, pale ivory sky. Early morning sunlight, delicate atmosphere, realistic farm scale, polished premium sustainability magazine mood. Compose a broad scenic panorama, with the hero subject on the right half and clear airy pale sky in the upper half. Authentic rural Nile Delta rather than European farm scenery. No writing, no logos, no charts, no text overlays, no watermarks. Landscape 16:9 aspect ratio.

All production CSS and JavaScript are local assets; external fonts, image CDNs and frontend frameworks are not required.
