# HASSAD Slide Content & Video Storyboard Specification

**Status:** authoritative English content specification • **Deck:** exactly 20 slides (15 main + 5 backup) • **Format:** 16:9 • **Presentation date:** 27-Jan-2027
**Project:** HASSAD (حصاد) — Agricultural Waste Digital Marketplace Website (MVP / Prototype) • **Tagline:** **From Seed → To Waste → To Reborn Value**

## 1. Non-negotiable production rules

- **Visual system:** warm ivory `#FAF8F5` canvas; deep agricultural green `#1B5E20`; leaf green `#388E3C`; terracotta `#8D5524`; harvest gold `#D4A017`; charcoal `#1F2933`; slate `#4A5568`; variance red `#C62828` only for rejection, top threats, or adverse variance. Dark text on the light canvas throughout.
- **Typography:** body text **≥24 pt**; titles 36–44 pt; hook headlines 40–52 pt; hero numbers 48–60 pt; diagram labels 18–22 pt only where necessary. Use one sans-serif family and semibold numerals. No body paragraph may be reduced to fit.
- **Content discipline:** one idea per slide; the numbered audience-facing copy below is final and contains **no more than six lines per slide**. Design labels, axes, legends, source footer, slide number, and the Act bar are metadata, not additional body copy.
- **Progress/navigation:** Slides 2–15 carry an evolving Botanical Growth Tracker—**ACT I • SEED & GAP | ACT II • DEEP ROOTS | ACT III • STEM & LEAVES | ACT IV • WEATHERING THE STORM | ACT V • HARVEST & REBORN VALUE**—with the current stage in gold, completed growth in green, and future growth in 15% green. Slide 1 introduces the complete Seed-to-Eco-Book arc; Slides 16–20 use an **APPENDIX** bar.
- **Numbering:** main slides use `01 / 15` through `15 / 15`; backup slides use `A1 / 5 • 16 / 20` through `A5 / 5 • 20 / 20`, always bottom-right.
- **Life cycle language:** use only **Predictive / Traditional**, Phase, Work Package, SRS, RTM, Scheduled Milestone, Daily Technical Coordination, CCB, approved baseline, and formal change control. Do not introduce terminology from any other delivery model.
- **Visual evidence:** redraw all charts and governance diagrams as native vector shapes. A website image must be either (a) a dated capture from the demonstrable prototype, labelled **PROTOTYPE CAPTURE**, or (b) a designed frame labelled **ILLUSTRATIVE MVP MOCKUP — NOT EXECUTION EVIDENCE**. Never imply that a mockup proves completion.
- **Motion:** use 0.4-second Fade between ordinary slides; use 0.6-second Push at Act changes; build charts by category, never by individual word. Keep the hook visible before revealing detail.

## 2. Source-of-truth and evidence protocol

### Locked precedence

1. The targeted five-member edit controls roster/order and the Seed-to-Value lifecycle. The master presentation prompt continues to control terminology, final dates, five official milestones, budget mathematics, approved completion scenario, and the 15+5 story architecture.
2. The thirteen artifacts below supply controlled baseline details and evidence. Instructor corrections override unreconciled draft wording. The locked end date is **27-Jan-2027**, not the older 24-Jan draft.
3. Artifact logs stop in Week 3. Any final result, Week-7 issue, contingency draw, closure EVM, or final quality outcome is **MODELED COMPLETION**, not verified historical evidence, until replaced by signed records.
4. The locked final-defense status is **4 Approved / 4 Deferred / 2 Rejected**. Reconciliation footnote: the 18-Nov-2026 source cut-off showed CR-009 as Pending; the locked completion narrative classifies it with the four deferred non-MVP requests.
5. The four approved enhancements add **EGP 14,000** to the Cost Baseline; they do **not** consume the risk reserve. Initial Cost Baseline **150,000** + approved adjustment **14,000** = revised BAC **164,000**. Add the unchanged reserve **50,000** = total authorized ceiling **214,000**.

### Evidence labels

- **[VERIFIED BASELINE]** — controlled charter/register/plan/schedule statement or approved decision as of the artifact cut-off.
- **[VERIFIED PROTOTYPE EVIDENCE]** — an evidenced early review, sign-off, communication, or prototype-related record actually present in the artifacts.
- **[MODELED COMPLETION]** — the approved graduation-defense completion scenario; verify against signed closure, ledger, test, and acceptance records before public use.
